# Code of Conduct

rv follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want rv's issues, pull requests, discussions, and Discord to be
welcoming for everyone, whatever their background, identity, or level of
experience. Be kind, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work,
not the person.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer,
[@christopherkarani](https://github.com/christopherkarani). Reports are
handled confidentially. Security vulnerabilities go through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily
ban, or permanently ban anyone who does. The full enforcement guidelines are
in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
